## Privacy Notice (KVKK)

### 1. Data controller

The data controller is <span lang="tr">Orçun Saatçi</span> as a natural person. You can send requests to the email address on the contact page. Other channel: `{{REQUEST_ADDRESS}}`.

### 2. Personal data processed and purposes

- **Hosting and CDN:** IP address, browser details, requested URL and timestamp are processed briefly to serve the site and keep it secure.
- **Web Analytics:** a cookieless hash derived from the request and discarded after 24 hours, the URL without query, referrer, country, operating system, browser and device type are processed for aggregate visit statistics.

### 7. Your rights (KVKK art. 11) and how to apply

Under KVKK art. 11 you have the right to:

- learn whether your personal data is processed;
- request information if it has been processed;
- learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly;
- know the third parties it is transferred to in Türkiye or abroad;
- ask for correction if it is incomplete or inaccurate;
- ask for deletion or destruction under art. 7;
- ask that corrections and deletions be notified to the third parties it was transferred to;
- object to an outcome against you that results solely from automated analysis;
- claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

If you are in the EU, you also have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection (GDPR arts. 15–21), and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Requests are answered within 30 days at the latest.

## Privacy Policy and Cookies

### Cookies and similar technologies

This site uses no cookies. Your browser stores only the preferences you choose:

- `os-theme` (localStorage): your theme preference, until you delete it.
- `os-motion` (localStorage): your motion preference, until you delete it.
- `os-sweep` (sessionStorage): whether the opening light sweep has played in this session; removed when the tab closes.

### Analytics and Global Privacy Control

Analytics are cookieless. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal, analytics do not run at all.

### Security

The site is served over HTTPS only, with HSTS and a Content Security Policy. There are no accounts, sessions or databases.

When this notice changes, the current version is published on this page and the “Last updated” date is refreshed.
