## Engineering and product development

I’m a computer engineer focused on mobile application development with Flutter. My work spans technical requirements, software architecture, implementation, and app store releases. I see mobile development as a discipline where design decisions and engineering inform one another. Understanding the purpose of a feature and how it fits into the wider product is an essential part of my process.

My product-focused approach starts with translating business requirements into practical technical decisions. I pay attention to implementation details while keeping the overall product in view, bringing ideas to life through thoughtful engineering.

## My approach to architecture

I structure Flutter projects using MVVM and Clean Architecture, with clear responsibilities across the codebase. Riverpod and Bloc help me manage application state and organize the relationship between interface behavior and business logic. I evaluate architectural choices against project scope, data flow, and maintenance needs, aiming for a structure that remains understandable as the application grows.

Clean Code principles guide my everyday work. Meaningful naming, explicit responsibilities, and consistent organization make a codebase easier to navigate. I want the code I write to be straightforward for teammates to understand and provide a dependable foundation for future changes.

## Integrations and the mobile experience

I work with REST and SOAP integrations, connecting mobile interfaces to the services and data they depend on. I consider the complete path from receiving a response to presenting information on screen. Clear boundaries between data handling, business logic, and presentation help keep these interactions manageable.

For interface development, I draw on Material 3, paying attention to visual consistency, clear interactions, and responsive behavior. Loading, empty, and error states are part of the experience I consider when building a screen. My focus is on making the technical implementation work coherently with the product’s design.

## From development to release

My end-to-end development experience includes publishing and release management on the App Store and Google Play. I consider how implementation decisions affect delivery and treat release preparation as part of the product lifecycle. This perspective helps me connect development work with the practical requirements of getting an application into people’s hands.

I also work with CI/CD processes and tools such as Docker to support consistent development environments and delivery workflows. Automating repetitive steps gives teams more room to focus on product requirements. I value tooling that addresses a clear need and makes day-to-day development more dependable.

## Collaboration and knowledge sharing

I believe software quality grows through shared engineering practices. Through code reviews, technical discussions, and mentoring, I contribute to common standards and exchange knowledge with teammates. Explaining the reasoning behind a solution helps the team make informed decisions when similar challenges arise.

Clear communication and constructive feedback are central to how I collaborate. I value discussing alternatives, understanding trade-offs, and sharing lessons from implementation. I bring the same perspective to developments in the Flutter ecosystem, evaluating new approaches by the problems they solve and their relevance to the project.
